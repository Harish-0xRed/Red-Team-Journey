## Lessons Learned

* A PCAP investigation should begin with the question, not with a random Wireshark filter.
* The investigation process is:
  * Question
  * What do I need to know?
  * What evidence can prove it?
  * Where can I find that evidence?
  * Which Wireshark protocol/field exposes it?
  * Verify the evidence
  * Record the conclusion
* MAC addresses can be identified from Ethernet II information associated with the suspected host.
* Different PCAP investigations may require different protocols and evidence paths.
* Practical Wireshark skill comes from understanding protocol fields and evidence relationships, not only memorizing display filters.
