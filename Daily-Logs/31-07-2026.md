## 💡 Lessons Learned

- `nc` communicates using plain TCP.
- `openssl s_client` communicates using SSL/TLS encryption.
- TLS encrypts data before it travels across the network.
- A server may present a digital certificate before encrypted communication begins.
- A self-signed certificate is expected in many lab environments and does not necessarily indicate a problem.
- Reading and understanding terminal output is as important as running the correct command.
