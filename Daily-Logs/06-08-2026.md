### Linux File Ownership

#### `chown` vs `chmod`
- Learned the fundamental difference between ownership and permissions.
- Understood that:
  - `chown` changes the owner and group of a file or directory.
  - `chmod` changes what users are allowed to do with a file or directory.
- Used real-world analogies to distinguish between changing the owner of a room and changing who is allowed to enter it.
