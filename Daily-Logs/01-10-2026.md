## Lessons Learned

* An expression evaluates to a single value.
* A statement performs an action, such as assigning a value to a variable.
* `str()` converts to a string, `int()` converts to an integer, and `float()` converts to a floating-point number.
* Comparison operators produce `True` or `False`.
* `and` means both conditions must be True.
* `or` means at least one condition must be True.
* `not` means the opposite.
* Boolean evaluation can be understood as: comparison → True/False → Boolean operator → final True/False.
